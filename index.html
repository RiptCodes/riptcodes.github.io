How they are made

  1. One moment first

    Every template starts as a hand-coded page built around one scroll moment, so the motion is exactly right before anything else.

  2. Then rebuilt in Framer

    Each section becomes a component with every text, photo, colour and speed in the right-hand panel, so you change it without touching code.

  3. Checked on small phones

    Every page is checked live at 1440, 390 and 320 pixels wide, and with reduced motion on, before it is listed.
